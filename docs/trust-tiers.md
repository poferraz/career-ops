# Evidence tiers

Career Ops uses four evidence tiers to classify the support behind career and labor-market claims. A tier label in a reference guide records the project's classification, not final proof: verify the underlying source and publication date before quoting a statistic or advising a job seeker.

| Tier | Evidence | How to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **T1 — Verified** | Peer-reviewed research, public primary data, or independently corroborated findings | Identify the study or dataset, the population studied, and the measured outcome. |
| **T2 — Reported** | An identified survey, platform analysis, or individual industry experiment | Attribute the result to its source and note relevant sample or method limits. |
| **T3 — Conventional** | Practitioner judgment, a writing technique, or a coaching framework | Present it as a technique to try, not settled empirical fact. |
| **T4 — Excluded** | Unverified, anecdotal, or contradicted claims | **Omit from advice.** If the question matters, state what evidence is missing. |

When sources disagree, compare their methods, dates, and relevance to the user's market. Live web research can surface current leads, but an independent check of the underlying evidence is still required.
