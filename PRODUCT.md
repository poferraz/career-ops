# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
High-performing job candidates, senior engineers, and career changers who need to craft hyper-compelling, evidence-based resumes, cover letters, and LinkedIn profiles that pass both automated ATS parsers (Workday, Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever) and human recruiter AI screening.

## Product Purpose
Career-Ops turns passive AI career advice into a deterministic, research-backed career operations engine. It eliminates generic AI fluff, enforces radical accomplishment density via Altworld/Hemmingway-1, naturalizes prose cadence and locks 100% of facts via jialinyyzz/humanizer, and verifies every deliverable against a 50-point Anti-Slop Rubric.

## Positioning
Unlike generic AI resume builders that produce high-probability chatbot cliches ("spearheaded cross-functional initiatives"), Career-Ops combines structured Motivational Interviewing (intake), dual-engine open-weight model synthesis, and deterministic factual invariant locking. No invented metrics, zero corporate fluff, 100% private and runnable locally.

## Operating Context
A candidate facing the 2026 hiring gauntlet: high-volume automated ATS semantic matching followed by a brutal 6-to-8-second human recruiter scan where 67% of reviewers reject resumes with visible "machine handwriting." The user operates a desktop-grade Three-Act Pipeline (Intake & Lock -> Studio Canvas -> Audit & Export) locally with zero API cost via Ollama or optionally through cloud endpoints.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Ingests PDF, DOCX, and Markdown resumes and extracts structured AST.
- Dual-Engine local pipeline: Stage 1 Hemmingway-1 (ChatML 27B) + Stage 2 Humanizer (Completion 12B).
- Invariant lock assertion: Numbers, dates, tools, and proper nouns are 100% invariant.
- Deterministic Anti-Slop scoring across 5 dimensions (Directness, Rhythm, Trust, Authenticity, Density) targeting >= 42/50.
- ATS single-column certified PDF and DOCX export.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: CAREER-OPS

## Product Principles
1. **Specification over evaluation**: Ask for verifiable facts, numbers, tools, and scope—never self-assessments.
2. **Invariants are law**: No metric, date, percentage, or proper noun may be rounded, hallucinated, or altered.
3. **Eliminate machine handwriting**: Natural human sentence burstiness and authentic phrasing defeat the 6-second recruiter AI sniff test.
4. **Privacy first**: Runs 100% locally on personal machines with zero cloud leaks of sensitive employment history.
